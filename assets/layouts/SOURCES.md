# Where the hole drawings come from

Every drawing in this folder was made by TeeTrip's own pipeline (`tools/layouts/`). Nothing was copied or traced from another golf app, a course website, a yardage book, or any commercial map or satellite service.

| What | Source | Licence | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hole lines, fairways, greens, tees, bunkers, water, woods, cart paths, buildings | OpenStreetMap contributors | Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0 | November 24, 2026 |
| Ground height along each hole | Natural Resources Canada, High Resolution Digital Elevation Model (HRDEM), Prince Edward Island 2020, 1 metre terrain model | Open Government Licence - Canada | November 24, 2026 |
| Par and yards | Each course's own published scorecard, as already shown on its TeeTrip page | Facts, credited on the course page | As on the page |

No aerial or satellite photographs were used. Google, Bing, Apple and Esri imagery were not used in any way.

The drawings are a guide to each hole, not a survey. Yardages are the course's own. A hole is only drawn when the mapped length from tee to green is within 8% of the scorecard; otherwise it is left out and marked "Layout coming".

Credit line to keep on every page that shows a drawing: "Shapes (c) OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Ground heights: Natural Resources Canada, HRDEM, PEI 2020 (Open Government Licence - Canada)."
