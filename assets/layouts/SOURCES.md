# Where the hole drawings come from

Every drawing in this folder was made by TeeTrip's own pipeline (`tools/layouts/`). Nothing was copied or traced from another golf app, a course website, a yardage book, or any commercial map or satellite service.

| What | Source | Licence | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hole lines, fairways, greens, tees, bunkers, water, woods, cart paths, buildings | OpenStreetMap contributors | Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0 | November 24, 2026 |
| Ground shading, tree lines, ground heights, and tee boxes the open map is missing | Natural Resources Canada, High Resolution Digital Elevation Model (HRDEM), Prince Edward Island 2020, 1 metre ground and surface models (LiDAR) | Open Government Licence - Canada | November 24, 2026 |
| Par and yards | Each course's own published scorecard, as already shown on its TeeTrip page | Facts, credited on the course page | As on the page |

No aerial or satellite photographs were used. The LiDAR is a height model, not a photograph: it gives the shape of the ground and how tall the trees are, not grass colour or mowing lines, so fairway outlines come only from OpenStreetMap. Google, Bing, Apple and Esri imagery were not used in any way.

Where the open map has no tee box at the scorecard's distance, the drawing uses the level, raised tee pad the LiDAR shows on the line of play, and only when that brings the hole within 8% of the scorecard.

The drawings are a guide to each hole, not a survey. Yardages are the course's own. A hole is only drawn when the mapped length from tee to green is within 8% of the scorecard; otherwise it is left out and marked "Layout coming".

Credit line to keep on every page that shows a drawing: "Shapes (c) OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Ground shading, tree lines and heights: Natural Resources Canada, HRDEM, PEI 2020 (Open Government Licence - Canada)."
